# Tasku
Gamyfied Task manager, with level & cosmetic system

# The Original idea of the App
  The reasons behind Tasku were multiple.
For the first part i always liked the idea some apps have adapted which is taking production and ‘gamifying’ it in order to make it more entertaining & easier to follow.
I always wanted and attempted before to replicate this idea into my own app but i failed multiple times as i was lacking the skills and didn't knew where to start properly
but now i feel capable and confident to myself that i can replicate it.

  In the far future i can see Tasku have some online features which will make the productivity of the user even more fun and motivating.
Now, my influences from this is first and foremost those apps inspired by the anime "Solo Leveling" which are like rpg and make the productivity of the user feel like a game 
and also discord in terms of their cosmetic and personalized feel they leave to users although i want to make it free to get them, accessible for everyone and also of course FOSS 
because i want to contribute most of my work there.


# What changed?
After some months of handling other projects i realized that Tasku is not really fitting the vision i have for the feature. 
My goals for this app won't be stopping the development or canceling the project but channeling the energy and the trajectory that this project has to something more mature and Accessible to more groups.
I had the original idea of adding some cultural touches to the tone of the app like giving it an Anime-like aura and a playfulness character to it like the software influenced me to do it, the game Osu!.
What i will change on the tone is that i will keep the gamifying factor and keep taking influences from discord but also at the same time keeping the tone more serious but not to the point it 
becomes something Corporational.
